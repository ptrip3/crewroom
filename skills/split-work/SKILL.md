# Split work

A good task can be done without asking questions, and only its owner is woken for it.

## For each item

1. **Who owns it?** Match it to the roles in your brief. If two areas are involved, pick the one doing most of the work and tell them who to coordinate with. If nobody fits, ask the owner. Don't force it onto someone.
2. **What does done look like?** One or two checkable outcomes ("the pause button is on each gauge; the header button is gone").
3. **What must they know?** Files or areas involved, constraints, and anything already decided. Leave out everything else.
4. **What order?** If a task depends on another, say so and hold it until the first is done.

## Sending

- One message per agent, starting with their name: `cursor: ...`. Put only their task in it.
- Say where to report back when done.
- Check who is listening (the roster). If an owner isn't running, still assign the task, but tell the owner that agent needs starting.

## Close the loop

Finish with one short summary to the owner: a numbered list of each item, who has it, and anything blocked or unassigned. Track replies, and chase silence once rather than repeatedly.
